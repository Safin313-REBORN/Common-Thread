PRODUCT TRANSPARENCY

Use and product limits

CommonThread is a hackathon workspace for evaluating a request-to-order workflow. Starter records and catalog data are illustrative and should be replaced with information your organization has reviewed.

Catalog and request status

Starter requests, locations, products, prices, availability, and restriction labels are not verified records, supplier offers, or guaranteed stock. Moving a request to Ready is an internal workflow decision; it does not verify a requester, prove eligibility, or establish that a need is genuine.

Payments and fulfillment

The current PayPal connection is sandbox-only. Captures go to the configured test merchant and do not move real money. Local order records send no payment. The app is not connected to live suppliers, recipient payouts, shipping, or delivery confirmation.

Planning recommendations

AI output is a product suggestion, not professional aid, medical, allergy-safety, financial, or procurement advice. Review each item and restriction. The server validates against its catalog and budget rules; it does not confirm that a product is safe or suitable for a recipient.

Operational scope

The app includes password-based accounts and workspace-scoped records, but it does not yet provide email verification, password recovery, MFA, invitations, managed backups, or production operations. The current PayPal connection is sandbox-only. The currency conversion is a daily reference estimate and may differ from PayPal's quote, fees, or settlement behavior. Do not expose this local build publicly or use it to coordinate real aid payments or sensitive records.
