PayPal checkout uses sandbox funds when credentials are configured. Local orders send no payment. The app has no live suppliers, delivery service, recipient payouts, or identity checks.

QUESTIONS

Before you build.

What does the AI decide?

Gemini ranks products from the supplied catalog and suggests quantities with a short reason. The server controls product IDs, prices, stock, constraints, and totals.

Can the agent place an order by itself?

No. You can edit or remove any item. PayPal checkout begins only after you choose the approved kit.

Is the catalog live from a supplier?

No. The workspace includes an illustrative starter catalog. Prices and availability are stored locally and are not supplier quotes or real inventory.

Will the sandbox order ship these items?

No. PayPal sandbox captures go to the configured test merchant and do not purchase or ship goods.

What goes to Gemini?

When configured, the server sends the selected need, your kit request, budget, restrictions, and available catalog details with storage disabled. No payment credentials are sent to the model.

Can I run it without API keys?

Yes. Local planning and no-charge order records work without Gemini or PayPal credentials. Add sandbox credentials to enable PayPal's test checkout.
